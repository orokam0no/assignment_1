Welcome to Old School Boxing Club

Old School Boxing Club is dedicated to pure, authentic boxing discipline. We focus on proper footwork, defensive mechanics, distance control, and effective striking power. Whether you are a beginner stepping into the ring for the first time or an active fighter preparing for sparring, our gym provides the traditional atmosphere and modern conditioning tools you need.


Authentic Boxing Experience


Why Train With Us

  • Classic Boxing Methodology: Master authentic stance, stance switches, slipping, and counter-punching.
  • Professional Ring & Equipment: Full-size boxing ring, heavy teardrop bags, speed bags, and double-end bags.
  • Experienced Coaching Staff: Headed by certified coaches with active amateur and professional boxing backgrounds.
