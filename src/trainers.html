Renat Latypov

Position
Branch manager and coordinator of the training staff
Experience
8 years

Certified fitness trainer with a background in functional training and PT exercise. He also studied dietetics and nutrition, and graduated from the "KazFitnessAcademy" fitness school. He created his own program called "Reasonable Fitness".

Over 8 years, Renat has worked with more than 300 clients. A client's health is his main priority.

Rustem Makishev

On-duty fitness trainer, specialist in fitness and bodybuilding. 5 years of experience as a trainer, and more than 10 years of sports practice overall. Works at the Uly Dala 55 branch.

Completed a personal trainer course, holds a CMS rank in boxing, and a rank in swimming. He works with clients using free weights up to 100+ kg.

He guarantees results, whether the goal is a better physique, better fitness, or more confidence.

Instagram: @rusleek

Evgenia Makoviy

Master trainer at Underground Gym. 4 years of experience as a trainer, 8 years of sports practice overall. Graduated with honors from Underground Academy, and holds a CMS rank in track and field.

Main focus: muscle gain, weight loss, physical therapy, and general fitness. She also works with clients who have spine problems (herniated discs, protrusions, osteochondrosis, scoliosis) and with knee, shoulder, or hip joint problems. She completed a first aid course.

Instagram: @eema_fitness
