Our Boxing Equipment & Training Zones

Our facility is equipped specifically for fight preparation, functional conditioning, and technical drilling.

Equipment & Facility Specifications

Heavy Bag & Agility Station

Professional Everlast heavy leather bags for power striking combinations and a floor agility ladder for footwork conditioning.

Functional Power Rig & Free Weights

Heavy-duty power rig equipped with barbells, bumper weight plates, kettlebells, and gymnastics rings for strength conditioning.

Cardio & Conditioning Ergometers

Rowing ergometers and GHD (Glute Ham Developer) benches for cardiovascular fight endurance and core strength development.

Conditioning Tools & Accessories

Wide selection of heavy medicine balls, dumbbells, wall balls, and climbing ropes for dynamic explosive power workouts.
